The plan

Three days a week, six workouts that rotate. Saturday, Monday and Wednesday. Every two weeks you have done all six.

Week A is push, pull, legs. Week B is Arnold's split: chest with back, shoulders with arms, then legs again with a glute and hamstring focus.

Machines and cables wherever they do the job as well as free weights, so setting up is quick and the form looks after itself.

How heavyThe last rep of each set should be hard but clean, with one or two reps left in the tank. Never grind or lose form.
ProgressWhen you hit the top of the rep range on every set, add a little weight next time. The app tells you when.
RestFollow the timer. Big lifts get 90 to 120 seconds, isolation gets 45 to 60. Resting properly lets you lift more.
Warm upDo the list every session. Before the first heavy lift, do one or two light sets too.
VideosEvery exercise has a short clip of a female trainer showing the movement. Watch it before your first set until it feels natural.
Busy gymIf a machine is taken, each exercise lists an alternative under the form cues.
Missed a dayJust do the next workout in the rotation on your next gym day. The order matters more than the calendar.
